About Me

I am a versatile and results-driven Software Engineering graduate from Çankaya University with strong hands-on experience in full-lifecycle software development, distributed architectures, and scalable systems. My track record spans high-performance web applications (React 19, TypeScript, Supabase), native mobile solutions (iOS, Swift, SwiftUI), and automated test suites (Python, Pytest, YOLO, OpenCV).

A major pillar of my engineering work is active aerospace research conducted in formal partnership with the TUSAŞ LIFT UP defense research program. Collaborating directly with flight test and software verification engineers, I engineered automated end-to-end verification platforms for Helicopter Cockpit Display Units (HDU) using computer vision, YOLO object detection, and automated OCR pipelines.

Across my professional internships at Mastersoft (developing real-time ML-driven telemetry visualization platforms) and Simtek Simulation Technologies (engineering deterministic C++17 simulation transaction engines with the Command Pattern), I have consistently championed clean code, modular software design, CI/CD automation, and modern development workflows. Whether building full-stack SaaS portals or native iOS apps with StoreKit 2 and CoreML, I focus on delivering robust, user-centric software that scales.
